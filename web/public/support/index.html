RacquetIQ — Support

RacquetIQ films a squash match, measures it on your phone, and hands the analysis back to you. If something is not working, or you have a question about your account or subscription, we are glad to help.

Contact us

Email prokaranvir@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version, and — if it is about a specific match — the approximate date and time you recorded it.

Common questions

  • Is my video private? By default, analysis runs entirely on your device and no video leaves your phone. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
  • How many analyses are free? Your first three analyses of your own videos are free. After that, RacquetIQ Pro unlocks unlimited analyses as an auto-renewable subscription.
  • How do I manage or cancel Pro? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then RacquetIQ. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, open the Account tab and choose Delete account. This removes your account from our servers.
